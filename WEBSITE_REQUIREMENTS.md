# Standing website requirements from Vivien

Apply this checklist whenever building or revising this website. It records the owner's broader website preference, restated on 8 September 2026.

1. Include a privacy policy, terms and conditions, cookie policy, refund policy and clear form consent. Tailor each to the actual website; do not invent business practices or policy terms.
2. Inspect cookies, browser storage, analytics, advertising trackers and third-party embeds. Establish whether optional technology needs consent. If optional tracking exists, provide meaningful acceptance, rejection and withdrawal before loading it.
3. Address accessibility: meaningful image alternatives, decorative image treatment, color contrast, keyboard access, focus, labels, validation, responsive text/reflow and understandable button labels. Respect device reduced motion. Per the owner's later request, omit the Reduce motion navigation button; the optional animation preference is available within Cookie preferences instead.
4. Remove fake reviews, unsupported claims and unverified numerical results or credentials. Preserve user-provided professional details while flagging what still needs substantiation.
5. Include known business details. Ask for missing legal/business identity information instead of fabricating it or publishing an inferred home address.
6. Check rights and licenses for all photos, images, fonts, logos and other content. Do not assume generated or online assets are automatically cleared.
7. Research potentially applicable local laws using authoritative sources and the actual activities, owner location and intended markets. Flag unresolved applicability rather than assuming universal compliance.
8. Deliver a review record distinguishing fixes, completed checks, unverified areas, risks and missing owner facts. Do not promise that no errors exist, that all risks have been found, or that nobody can sue.

Keep prior designs in version history. Changes should be visible in the requested deliverable; clearly identify whether a revision is saved, privately published or public. The access policy remains owner-only unless the user requests a different audience.

Use the originally selected first Interwoven FVA monogram unchanged in the menu bar. Remove dash separators in website copy and decorative divider lines; retain clear control boundaries and accessible focus indicators.
